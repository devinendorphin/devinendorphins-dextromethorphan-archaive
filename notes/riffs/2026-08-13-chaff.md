## Reconstruction

**The design.** I wanted an inverse of the structure of a triangulation — which
is one person in control of the information between two people. I wanted to put
out a **cloud of chaff** of various POVs, gleaned from Quora, Reddit, and the
study on psychosis and schizophrenia by the British Psychological Society.

**Where the BPS document came from.** I found it when I was processing the
hurtful act of a friend I had taken care of a time or two before — [who acted]
in response to an angry email regarding my ex, who had continued to be part of
the community and was advertising it on their feed. I was angry at the unspoken
year-long harm of refusing to listen, and almost [?Bank→banking] on my
dissolution, because it was more valuable to be validated by my abusers. It was
honest and justified and I still stand by it.

**The email was already broadcast.** I made sure I did not email just my ex. I
emailed the people who were part of the accountability team for MIX, and mutual
friends who are not my abusers but who certainly didn't come support in the
ensuing months.

**Why broadcast, not one-to-one.** Not knowing who might be compromised — who
might have been approached, and be rooting for the side convinced about my smear
campaign — I felt the multiplicity distinguished me from the narrative they were
portraying. **They were expecting me to go to each of these people and state my
case in exactly the way they had been primed to expect, as the product of my
substance use.** Instead I blitzed their feed.

**And the loss of control was part of the design.** It would have been a blitz if
it were 2014, but it was 2019 — I don't know who saw what, and in fact I think
that was also part of the structure: **it was to be a volley of information that
I would not be able to control.**

**The adversarial IM thread.** Especially since it contained an adversarial
instant-messenger discussion with a very old friend. On the same day I was
finally to transfer my duties to another person, at a bank, he suddenly messaged
me saying he was going to expose me for the abuser I am — and proceeded to do so
on the festival Facebook profile. My cell signal at the bank prevented me from
even seeing it or doing anything about it, so I had to let go and let it happen.
My response was: *oh my God, thank God you're here, this is the perfect moment
you could have done this, let's get into it.* And so we had a big argument.

**The preamble.** Two friends of ours had come to me concerned about his
growingly unstable state. It's not his first time; it seems to come in three-to
four-year cycles, and usually involves an almost chronological engagement with
friends from elementary school to middle school to high school, ending with us.
The previous time was 2013, when he called me frantically because the cops were
about to take him away — the type of moment one would be totally ape-shit about,
because *oh my God, are they going to kill him, are they going to shoot him.*
That was 2013. This was 2019. We had it out.

**Consent.** I asked him, when things had settled and we got into some honest
talk, if it was okay for me to put this up on my feed, and he said yes. Since
then we've spoken, and I hope he's okay.

**Why it is in the piece.** It seems that cycle is the result of abuse that was
rather systemic, at the intersection of school and religious community, and
experienced by many of his friends too. I'm saying this to **fortify the thesis
of the British Psychological Society's document** — not to put my friend on
blast. I haven't said his name, and I blocked his name out.

## What this corrects

### 1. "Chaff", not "simultaneity" — and the word is a countermeasure

`FACEBOOK.md` attributed the mechanism to **simultaneity**: everyone holding the
same record at once, so no tailored private version survives. That was my word
and it is the wrong shape.

His is **chaff** — the radar countermeasure. A cloud of reflectors does not
out-argue the incoming missile or prove anything to it. **It makes the target
unresolvable by multiplying returns.** You do not win by being believed; you win
by being impossible to lock onto.

That is exactly the quantum-tiger image from 2019-05-30 turned around for
defence. There he described the *perpetrator* as several flickering copies that
cannot be shot straight on. Six weeks later he becomes that: *"I felt the
multiplicity distinguished me from the narrative they were portraying."*
`MAY2019.md` reads the quantum tigers as a model of the abuser and an epistemic
claim about method. It is also a **template he then adopted.**

### 2. The one-to-one channel was pre-poisoned, which is why broadcast was forced

The sharpest thing in the riff, and it reframes the whole operation:

> They were expecting me to go to each of these people and state my case in
> exactly the way they had been primed to expect, as the product of my substance
> use.

The obvious move — talk to each person, explain — was **already written into the
narrative about him**. Making it would have confirmed it. The smear did not just
occupy the one-to-one channel; it *predicted his use of that channel* and turned
the use itself into evidence.

So broadcast was not chosen because it reaches more people. It was the only
channel whose use the script did not already interpret. `FACEBOOK.md`'s account —
simultaneity defeats tailoring — is a claim about coverage. The real constraint
was **that any expected move confirms the story, so the move has to be one the
story does not predict.**

### 3. The uncontrollability was intentional, which reverses my last correction

An hour before this riff I wrote that the collapse of organic reach left the
rebuttal "competing against private messaging with a throttled broadcast
channel", and called it a structural disadvantage that better explains failure
than the argument's merits.

He says the opposite, and he is describing intent rather than consolation:

> it would have been a blitz if it were 2014, but it was 2019 — I don't know who
> saw what, and in fact I think that was also part of the structure: it was to be
> a volley of information that I would not be able to control.

**Triangulation is total information control. The exact inverse is not
broad control — it is relinquished control.** Chaff works precisely because the
person deploying it cannot say which reflector any given observer sees. A
rebuttal whose distribution he could direct would have been another controlled
channel, structurally the same object as the thing he was countering.

So the degraded feed was not the obstacle. It was the medium the design required,
and I had it backwards. The correction stands in `FACEBOOK.md`.

### 4. The adversarial screenshots were published with consent, obtained after the fact

`FACEBOOK.md` reads the inclusion of messages attacking him as evidence the dump
was not one-sided, and infers the motive from the audience — being seen to
publish the unfavourable half is what makes the record admissible. That holds,
but it understates what happened. He asked the other party afterwards, once
things had settled, and was told yes.

It also names the ethical frame: the friend's material is included **to fortify
the BPS thesis** — that the crisis was a response to systemic abuse at the
intersection of school and religious community, shared by many of that friend's
peers — rather than to expose a person. Hence no name, and the name blacked out.

### 5. The BPS report is the spine of the July material

*Understanding Psychosis and Schizophrenia* (British Psychological Society,
Division of Clinical Psychology) is the source of screenshots the July OCR pass
identified only by content: *"Therapists' responsibility is to radical
collaboration and acceptance"*; *"Many people find that 'antipsychotic'
medication helps […] However, there is no evidence that it corrects an underlying
biological abnormality"*; and the long passage ending *"her voices are more than
just voices. They are many different selves, with different names, ages,
experiences, feelings, identities; dissociated selves that became internal
representations of her external world."*

`FACEBOOK.md` credited the survivor-multiplicity valence to "a quoted source" and
`MAY2019.md` later showed he had his own version six weeks earlier. **The quoted
source now has a name, and it is a clinical-establishment document arguing that
psychosis is a response to adversity rather than a brain disease.** That is why
the drug-war history, the critical psychiatry and the personal material are
interleaved rather than separated: they are one argument, and the BPS report is
its authority.

The other stated sources are **Quora and Reddit** — which accounts for the
register mix that `fb_jul2019.py` could not classify, and confirms the chaff was
assembled from deliberately heterogeneous provenance rather than curated for
quality.

### 6. A small amendment on the redaction

`FACEBOOK.md` says what is blacked out is *"not a name but a recurring category
term"*. He says: *"I haven't said his name, and I blocked his name out."* Both
are true — the category term is struck throughout the clinical material, and at
least one personal name is struck in the message screenshots. The OCR scan found
zero personal names across 106,030 characters, which is consistent with both.

## The one thing worth pushing on

The design is coherent and I think it is right about its own logic. The part that
does not close is this: **chaff defeats a lock. It does not hold a formation.**

The stated aim was retaining the social net. Chaff makes you unresolvable to
whoever is targeting you — and it makes you equally unresolvable to everyone
else, including the people whose continued presence was the point. A cloud of
heterogeneous POVs from Quora, Reddit and a clinical report, 157 fragments in
four days, with the diagnostic label struck out and no names, is *by construction*
hard to summarise, side with, or repeat to a third party. The properties that
defeat the smear are the same properties that make the material hard to be
persuaded by.

Which may be why the measurable trace runs the way it does. The **written** half
— linear, named, arguable — coincides with the highest month of new friendships
in five years (nine in May 2019, rank 3 of 118, all still friends in 2024). The
**chaff** half leaves no measurable trace in either direction.

That is not evidence the chaff failed; `FACEBOOK.md` is explicit that reach and
retention are both unmeasurable here, and a zero month is unremarkable. But if
the two halves were doing different jobs, the archive can only see the job the
prose was doing, and that is the job that looks like it worked.
