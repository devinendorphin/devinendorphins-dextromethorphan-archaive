# What ends a retry run?

Generated by `analysis/stopping.py`.

**13,336** branch points where at least one generation was re-rolled and the branch resolved.

| resolution | branches | share |

| human wrote it | 6,944 | 52.1% |

**52%** of re-roll events end with you writing it yourself rather than accepting any generation. That is the single largest category of 'rejection' in the corpus, and it is invisible to a chosen/rejected pair set, which by construction only sees the branches where a generation eventually won.
