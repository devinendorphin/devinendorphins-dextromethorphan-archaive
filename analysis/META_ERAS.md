# META_ERAS — characterization across time

*Quantitative passes: `analysis/meta_eras.py` (posts/year, median length/year,
log-odds distinctive vocabulary per era, with a random-halves control).
Reading pass: sampled posts from each era, quoted with local dates and URLs.
All dates America/New_York.*

Six eras. The boundaries are drawn where posting volume, median length, and
distinctive vocabulary all move together — and where the reading pass confirms
the voice actually changes. The 2012 and 2017 zeros are real gaps in the
record, not fetch artifacts (`META_EXPORT.md` §"Coverage gaps").

## Era 1 — The Status-Update Years, 2008–2012

21–62 posts/year, all Facebook, all text, median 46–115 characters. The
distinctive vocabulary is pure collegiate mundanity: *plane, resume, books,
weather, eating, cleaning, rommie* (log-odds z 4.4–5.8 against the rest of the
corpus; the random-halves control tops out near |z|≈3, so this structure is
not noise).

The voice is third-person Facebook-status voice — the platform's old default,
kept after the default changed:

> "is processing the ramifications of his bubble bursting." (2008-10-28,
> https://facebook.com/723125492/posts/pfbid0noHzKKLVTwXGUWemyZx5XkJP7XSCyhDGrfitmueKZTXNtLpFKo9Mi8DeC7CRVBael/)
>
> "feels this would be the proper time for any visitors of perpendicular cosmi
> to come forward and absurdly twist the outcome of today, because Devon is a
> dorkwad." (2008-11-04 — the day of the 2008 election,
https://facebook.com/723125492/posts/pfbid0NAHAKQdVkKDuR9L1zDKwQ86XvodPq9t6hd1ppkoXTk8J3pTFSXeVsFyL3ti97wudl/)
>
> "has new year's doldrums, but is excited at the notion of making sushi with
> rommie." (2009-01-02,
https://facebook.com/723125492/posts/pfbid02Tca2tqaDZx1cGmWfzyZd4esPt9cHKGxijhFYHmCuaPCZqjzSqYKWEk9bmktWtHel/)

Dorky, self-deprecating, domestic. Politics appears as a punchline, not a
preoccupation. Nothing in this era addresses power; the closest the corpus
gets to coercion is a head shaver that "whines at the work" (2009-08-01,
https://facebook.com/723125492/posts/pfbid0Eb9Q4UqimsKeVZRJg15iWkTVC7jUE98tzAv79Cv8Zsk4Y3CebyfCHyme2sbDkud4l/).

## Era 2 — The Thin Years, 2013–2018

0–20 posts/year, with literal zeros in 2012 and 2017. Median length stays
short. Distinctive terms: *window, laptop, internet, keys, glass, failing,
anymore, neighbors, colleagues* — objects and stuckness. The voice is flatter,
more anomic:

> "MENTAL NOTE: if you try to reach me tomorrow onward and get no answer.
> That's because my phone minutes ran out." (2013-05-23,
https://facebook.com/723125492/posts/pfbid02c9ASAzdwTihfvTSxWQbEsJnyEUNcJ16UkaGAdztPZ3joGFVKdQWsNXQEoG2C5m9Kl/)
>
> "Postpartum Decompression" (2013-11-27 — the whole post,
https://facebook.com/723125492/posts/pfbid0Vgga1GYuWgaqedqaiUKXLhcGNDDfC6AbA9iMHhvYp9dDPiQd57Zo8MAt1Fz33r6nl/)
>
> "Kicking the last underwear, but relieved that I mean business. -- DevonBot"
> (2015-04-07,
https://facebook.com/723125492/posts/pfbid0Tin1jtMyDz5MBAJhjLZ29ojUHpDpnwfyhCTL2hzKk3PY8NydCmT8HAL4PtGuFm4Yl/)

"DevonBot" is doing a lot of work in that 2015 post: the self as a process to
be automated, and the first hint of the machine-facing voice that will
dominate a decade later. This is the era the corpus says least about — which
is itself the characterization: a life mostly not being posted.

## Era 3 — Return and COVID, 2019–2020

110 then 166 Facebook posts; median length jumps to 239 characters in 2020.
Instagram restarts (33 posts in 2019). Distinctive terms: *louder, redacted,
partner, virus, covid, social*.

Two things arrive at once: a relationship and a pandemic, and the writing
braids them. The "louder" posts are housing-and-recovery advocacy
("Housing and recovery are intertwined, and don't make me say it louder and
louder and louder…", 2019-11-15,
https://facebook.com/723125492/posts/pfbid029GQbQKJgqn6j2wnmyc84muR2P3H46SE4spWbe54HQK7W19aijHEis9WAppWNzCLCl/). The "redacted" posts are a late-2020
sequence of named-and-redacted interpersonal conflict — a scammy Venmo
transaction, a public letter to "Satan," a test of who is keeping tabs on the
feed (2020-10-13 to 2020-10-17). The interpersonal and the systemic are
already being held in the same hand:

> "please stop tying your wellbeing to official announcements. please assume
> the worst and prepare accordingly… you are the latest generation in billions
> of years of survival. you are state-of-the-art biologic" (2020-03-17,
https://facebook.com/723125492/posts/pfbid0b58gD6r7aUmkadAmGFgoVDJQThpZMPjq9X1icjiJLrRVXnVqNoau6S6UrMENkCAbl/)

The voice is longer, urgent, second-person, sermon-adjacent. This is the first
era where other people's suffering is a regular subject (harm reduction,
overdose, shelters).

## Era 4 — The Serial Projects, 2021–2022

The prolific turn: 314/347 Facebook posts, 481/632 Instagram posts, and the
arrival of video (47/58 FB videos, 115/144 IG reels). Median lengths hold
~150–175 characters, but the distribution has a long tail of very long
captions. Distinctive terms: *style, imagine, meth, latent, moods, image,
hegemony, self*.

This is the era of **serialized AI-assisted writing projects**, named and
numbered:

- **DAAM** (Domestic Abuse Awareness Month, October 2021) — near-daily
  model-generated domestic-abuse narratives ("Quora, NovelAI… present: The
  Domestic User"): "DAAM 3 — …an aggregate of
  every abuse narrative ever posted on quora, reddit… digested by natural
  language processors into a single definitive abuse narrative" (2021-10-03,
https://facebook.com/devon.gallegos/videos/304398304352978/).
- **MTDS** ("Meth's True Dark Side," September 2021, National Recovery Month)
  — 100+ numbered installments on methamphetamine and harm reduction:
  "MTDS 3 — False, in that I have written a lot about the shitty and
  manipulative things users do to each other, especially when intimate."
  (2021-09-06,
  https://facebook.com/723125492/posts/pfbid0p2PYXFfmqDW4m6BPkrgmoppEf7yzGFRxutAz86R7nnMMbUhA74UVwmuVueuxLJDZl/)
- The numbered smear-campaign series ("1 — How to spot the inverts who start
  the smear campaigns.", 2021-06-25,
  https://facebook.com/723125492/posts/pfbid0uKbcMMjoLRiQwtbWiAygFTVBhxCpQSmJmeTQvzVmj15XXcwz35ERmBrhE8bQhbBXl/),
  the "hall of mirrors" hookup post
  (2022-06-24,
  https://facebook.com/723125492/posts/pfbid027xR8Qz97UKeG2WuwYyLahjBur3cjzEnD4LhkqL6dpjbKXoHfcgkAY4HxtVHmpJLl/), the 2022-05-26 run of lowercase NYC-apocalypse satires
  ("nyc that is now run by the metastisized met gala whose theme this century
  is dance of the seven surveils"
(https://facebook.com/723125492/posts/pfbid0oi3NmyEY2Y4DeTPTGVboX4P8oY5XFXHf624Q4qT5YRJjDEec19KKsqTTxcyCXUKHl/)).

The voice is maximalist, polyphonic, often lowercase, swinging between
surrealist image-dumps ("pulsing everything languorous formless forms…",
2022-05-28,
https://facebook.com/723125492/posts/pfbid02tvZ37NbC1xcoUM2qbeGVumALy5mArZvE7eacbTbcPj2fcUkxCiDg7irsRH7Hpw5Jl/) and direct-address confessionals. Interpersonal violence is now a
central, explicit subject — and it is being *generated with* language models,
which is the bridge to the next era. Note the date: the DAAM series is
October 2021, the same year the NovelAI corpus in this repo is at full tilt.
The two archives are the same practice on different surfaces.

## Era 5 — Counterfactual Intelligence, 2023–2024

Volume falls back (233/216 FB, 71/94 IG); the center of gravity moves to
video essays and LLM experiments. Distinctive terms: *it's, i'm, dude, shot,
extreme, yaacov, gpus, system, stablediffusion* — the "yaacov" cluster is
Stable Diffusion prompt captions (Yaacov Agam, 2022-08-31), and the
contractions mark a more spoken, vlog-adjacent register.

Named projects: **The Counterfactual Interview** (interrogating simulated
powerful figures), **The Sydney Bing Show** (jailbroken-model dialogues,
2023-05), and the recurring "Full Value of Human Data" argument — that human
creative data is training-data value owed compensation (2024-05-10
https://facebook.com/723125492/posts/pfbid021m6W7FHvXDGv7yo74ox9JZkaM3EV8qYsvNh5fTpaMd4guNCr7oAUue7b9oPTHka2l/): "If
artists were to accept a one-time compensation payment of say $10,000 for
their works being used to train AI systems… they would potentially be losing
out on orders of magnitude more value").

The day job enters the frame: Uber Eats delivery by subway and on foot,
"three avenues and five streets… from Houston… to midtown" (2024-01-18,
https://facebook.com/723125492/posts/pfbid02HdX3pZqafCNi4ToCmkv8p94dLjsdGBX3Shz8PixPXuwtckWaWYVi1wb42uD3a9uSl/);

(2023-12-12,
https://facebook.com/723125492/posts/pfbid0ea3a2RWGeiGipskKw97GvHgpdEfQFL8AYvZ31heTjCkjviwcHUHFgSiPNr5TBh5Ql/). And the political register sharpens after the 2024 election:

The voice is essayistic, often very long, structured with headers and
numbered points — the caption as white paper.

## Era 6 — The Alignment Diaries, 2025–2026

Distinctive terms, the strongest in the whole corpus (z up to 12.0):
*claude, episode, alignment, comments, model, link, youtube, gemini, openai,
chatgpt, conversation, project, fable*. 49 of 61 Facebook posts in 2026 carry
video; the Instagram story archive swells (120 items in 2026).

The subject is now **the models themselves, as interlocutors and as readers**:

> "this conversation was less about humans reading it and more about seeing if
> models can handle emotional abuse (like if they can grok the meanings at the
> backstage of language, and ooohhhhhhhh boy can they!)" (2023-11-06 — the
> turn happens here, at the era boundary,
> https://facebook.com/723125492/posts/pfbid0YZECnLXDH6KAgkv6TqsDKJrYswnRexU2yjUXgMqfn2ths4xu1Cewm8NsZN1qtmdwl/)
>
> "This is inference for a chatbot… Its reward function I trust more than a
> human's incentive, the incentive that buries these histories. For now."
> (2025-12-31,
https://facebook.com/723125492/posts/pfbid0CMQPHiDTsZmZSGNJEDme26FHrySSVKeTwA7HYji4k5fExYkPLZLs4nzm9wKMAXBkl/)
>
> "When it comes to the strongman personality, the Goldwater rule can suck
> it. #badfaith #strongman #fascism #abuse" (2026-03-13,
https://facebook.com/reel/3897039707093124/)

Named series: **Myths for Now** (2025-06-19: "for the audience of the future
is a metaphor"), the **PFCizer** management-training parodies, **Local
repair** (the understanding "forged with your digital entity," 2026-08-01).
The political and the model-critical have fused: alignment is now the lens for
everything, including the strongman.

The era's thesis, stated outright in the corpus's single most fourth-wall
caption (see `META_FOURTHWALL.md`,
https://www.instagram.com/p/DdoOzX3oKlF/):

> "…your monitor almost done with the 52 agents you assigned to read over
> every conversation you had with this language model to map out all the
> times it lied to you and how." (2026-09-23)

He is writing, by the end, for the analysts — human or otherwise — he knows
are coming.

## What the eras share

Across all six: the lowercase register that arrives in Era 3 and never fully
leaves; the numbered series as a compositional habit (Era 4's DAAM/MTDS,
Era 6's episodes); the move from writing *about* his life to writing *with*
models about power. And one constant the numbers confirm: the intimate and
the systemic were never separate topics for him. The phrase meditation
(`META_PHRASE.md`) traces that braid year by year.
