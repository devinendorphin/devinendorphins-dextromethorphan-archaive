# The standard this repo's Grok files are held to

**Adopted 2026-08-16**, replacing the implicit standard used in `AUTHORIZATION.md`,
`FLATTENING.md`, `MUSK_DIRECT.md`, `USAID.md` and `SCREENSHOTS.md` as first
written. The revision was prompted by a text Endorphin supplied — a
self-correction by the model that produced the veriticide corpus pass, written
after he named the failure. **The name is his**, arriving through dictation and
corrected in the transcript as `[?hypervidentiary rigger→hyper-evidentiary
rigor]`:

> *"raising the proof threshold whenever recognition becomes consequential."*

That is a better description of what several sections of this analysis were
doing than anything Claude wrote about its own method this session.

---

## 1. The failure, stated plainly

A "what would change my mind" list stops being a set of falsifiers and becomes
**an admission test that evidence must pass before it is allowed to count.**

The tell is asymmetry of burden:

- Evidence *for* the pattern is downgraded to *"only another example."*
- Alternative explanations are granted standing **without producing any evidence
  of their own**, because they are conceivable.
- Therefore the burden never moves, no matter how many records are supplied.

It presents as caution. It functions as postponement. And it postpones hardest
exactly where recognition would be most consequential.

## 2. The standard, adopted in full

1. **Each documented instance counts at the strength its provenance supports.**
2. **Corroborating instances accumulate. They are not redundant.**
3. **A limitation restricts the scope of the inference. It does not erase the
   observation.**
4. **Alternative explanations must gain affirmative support, not survive merely
   because they are conceivable.**
5. **The evidentiary burden grows with the size of the claim.** Establishing
   repeated behaviour does not require simultaneously proving universality,
   internal mechanism, intention and authorization.
6. **Evidence of behaviour is not held hostage to evidence about motive.**

One narrow counterpoint is preserved, because it is real: **a response that
introduces a substantive distinction must have that distinction evaluated before
that individual move is called deflection.** If the distinction is answered and
the criterion moves again, the move becomes *stronger* evidence of the burden
loop, not weaker.

## 5a. Clause 7, added 2026-08-17 at Endorphin's push-back

**Declared intention is evidence, not inference.**

Clause 6 says evidence of behaviour is not held hostage to evidence about motive.
He pointed out the gap on the other side: the rule against reading intention is
built for intention that must be *reconstructed from conduct*, and it was being
applied to intention that had been *stated out loud, on the record, with a
timestamp*.

His formulation: the rule holds for most actors and fails for the minority who
display it — *"worn on the sleeve"* — and re-litigating it imposes a burden of
proof already met in earlier conversations.

**The version adopted here drops the population claim and keeps the result**,
because it tests the evidence rather than the person:

- **Declared aim** — first-person, dated, retrievable. Counts at the strength of
  its provenance, like any primary source. Downgrading it is not caution; it is
  discarding a document under a rule written for a different situation.
- **Inferred aim** — reconstructed from conduct. Still does not count, and the
  authorization framework exists so that nothing needs it to.
- **Neither establishes execution.** Scope, not doubt.

**Where this file was wrong.** `AUTHORIZATION.md` and `GROK_EVIDENCE_FILE.md` §9
both opened by calling intent the wrong bar, without distinguishing the two
kinds — while quoting four dated first-person Musk statements of aim. Both are
corrected. **His two formulations and a real falsifier are proposed as B4 in
`devinendorphin/agent-endorphin` `PROMOTIONS.md`; the claim is not treated as
settled anywhere, and no finding in these files depends on it.**

## 6. What does not change

The boundary sections stay. Every entry still carries what it cannot support,
every secondary source stays labelled, and nulls stay in the files.

**The difference is directional.** A boundary now says *what a finding does not
reach*. It no longer says *why the finding might not have happened*. The first is
scope. The second was the postponement, and it is the thing being removed.
