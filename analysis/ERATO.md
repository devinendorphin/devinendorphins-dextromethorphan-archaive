# The Erato era

Generated by `analysis/erato.py`.

**374** generating stories, 52.9M characters of output, November 2024 to July 2026. Llama 3 70B, settings schema v7.

## Temperature is not in the pipeline

Of 374 generating Erato stories, **227 (61%)** do not have `temperature` in their enabled sampler order at all. The Unified sampler replaces it.

In **226 of 227** of those, the stored temperature is exactly **1** — the neutral value, sitting unused in a field that is not part of the pipeline. When temperature *is* enabled the median is **1.37**.

This is Erato-only. Every other model in the corpus has temperature enabled in 100% of its stories:

| model | stories | temperature absent from the order |
|---|---:|---:|
| `kayra-v1` | 1,046 | 0.0% |
| `llama-3-erato-v1` | 374 | 60.7% |
| `clio-v1` | 136 | 0.0% |
| `euterpe-v2` | 94 | 0.0% |
| `krake-v2` | 76 | 0.0% |
| `glm-4-6` | 62 | 0.0% |

**Consequence.** Erato's headline 'median temperature 1.00' is an artifact of reading an inert field. Any comparison that uses it — including `FINDINGS.md` §9's argument that Erato was run cool while Euterpe and Krake were run hot — is comparing a live setting against a disabled one.

## The border, on the Unified sampler

Same measure as `FINDINGS.md` §4c: `wordfreq` Zipf frequency splits alphabetic tokens into non-words (zero English frequency, the noise floor) and real-but-rare words (Zipf < 2.5, the register climbing). Generations of 60+ tokens.

| preset | generations | linear | quad | non-word % | rare word % | rare : non |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| `goldenarrow` | 2,200 | 0.500 | 0.19 | 0.53 | 0.86 | **1.63** |
| `zanyscribe` | 2,200 | -0.275 | 0.35 | 2.45 | 3.24 | **1.32** |
| `shousetsuka` | 1,236 | 0.300 | 0.34 | 2.02 | 2.06 | **1.02** |
| `dragonfruit` | 2,200 | 0.900 | 0.07 | 1.10 | 1.11 | **1.01** |
| `wilder` | 2,200 | 0.000 | 0.19 | 1.23 | 1.03 | **0.84** |

Against the classic stack, from §4c:

| configuration | non-word % | rare word % | rare : non |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| classic, hot, truncation first | 0.96 | 1.58 | 1.65 |
| classic, hot, temperature first | 2.00 | 1.46 | 0.73 |
| classic, cool | 0.62 | 1.10 | 1.77 |
| Unified, `goldenarrow` | 0.53 | 0.86 | 1.63 |
| Unified, `zanyscribe` | 2.45 | 3.24 | 1.32 |

**No Erato preset beats the classic stack's exchange rate.** The best of them, `goldenarrow`, lands at 1.63 against the 1.65 of high temperature with truncation running first — a tie, near enough — and it gets there at a much lower absolute level of both quantities (0.86% rare against 1.58%). Cleaner, but it does not reach as far.

**What the Unified sampler does add is range.** `zanyscribe` (linear -0.275, quad 0.35) reaches a rare-word rate of **3.24%** — roughly double anything the classic stack achieved at any temperature — at 2.45% non-words, the highest noise floor measured anywhere in the corpus. Its exchange rate of 1.32 is well above the classic stack's naive hot setting (0.73) and well below its best.

So the Unified sampler **extended the range without improving the exchange rate**. Temperature-plus-truncation could not get the register that high at any price; `math1` could, and the price was a noise floor two and a half times the corpus norm. The border did not disappear — it moved outward and stayed just as steep.
