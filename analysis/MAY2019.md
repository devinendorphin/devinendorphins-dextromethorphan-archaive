# MAY 2019 — the written half

2026-08-13. Companion to the July 2019 screenshot pass in `analysis/FACEBOOK.md`.
Source and method: `analysis/FB_EXPORT.md`. Not held to the `FINDINGS.md`
measurement standard; this is a reading.

The summer 2019 series has two halves. July is documentary and almost entirely
images — 157 image-only posts in four days, analysed in `FACEBOOK.md`. **May is
the written half**, and no word-count pass missed it; what was missed is what it
contains.

He anonymises throughout, unprompted: *the seven*, *cognitive styles*,
*Person / Other / Third*. There are no third-party names in the May posts. The

## The Counterfactual Interview is invented here, by hand

On **2019-05-23** he posts a 3,030-word document headed:

> Transcript 1: **noveD sogellaG** — Interview with Devon Gallegos — 5-23-19 — L
> Train, Williamsburg, All stops between — G train, Kensington — Dunkin Donuts by
> Church Ave

`noveD sogellaG` is his own name reversed. The piece is a two-column dialogue
between DEVON and NOVED, and **NOVED is adversarial**: it interrupts, mocks
(*"Sickies."*), presses for specifics, refuses vague answers, and at one point
flatly contradicts him (*"False. Fake. That it's not true. That's what I
meant!"*).

He states the purposes outright, in the transcript, in NOVED's second question:

1. *"Illustrate the trope of splitting of single self"* — citing VALIS, *A
   Scanner Darkly*, and *Limitless*.
2. *"Replicate and critique a genre of youtube victim recovery video and make one
   in that style that doesn't replicate potential harms in the act of narration
   and closure."*
3. *"Go back to my roots when I used to do auto-FAQs for my AOL memberpage."*

**This is `READINGS.md` §IV's Counterfactual Interview — the form §IV calls the
corpus's most functional part and "a technique for the powerless" — running
fifteen months before AI Dungeon and with no model involved.** He convenes an
interlocutor, gives it a name and a hostile disposition, and interviews himself
under pressure. Point 3 dates the practice further back still: auto-FAQs on an
AOL member page, which would put a rough ancestor of the form in the late 1990s.

The implication for the corpus is not small. §IV, and `READINGS.md`'s framing
generally, treat the convened-interlocutor form as something that emerged in
dialogue with language models. **The form predates the models.** What the models
supplied was a second voice he no longer had to write himself.

## The refusal of the label is stated in May and executed in July

`FACEBOOK.md` records that the July screenshots black out a recurring *category
term* everywhere it occurs, mid-sentence included, leaving the mechanism and
removing the diagnosis. The reasoning is given two months earlier, in the
transcript:

> **NOVED:** Can you be more specific?
> **DEVON:** No, the general terms for these styles have a lot of baggage. I'm
> not a fan of any label offered in the DSM, and the very fact it comes from the
> DSM is a problem. Like, my sexuality was in there in the 70s. When a human
> construct like that is pathologized it can also be stigmatized. […] But when
> I'm my best selves, Disorders are seen as just different Orders.

So the black bars are not redaction for privacy in the ordinary sense and not a
legal precaution. **They are the May position applied with a marker.** The
argument was designed from the outset to run without the category, and in July he
enforced that on his own sources.

The same passage carries *"when I'm my best selves"* — multiplicity turned on
himself, in May, before either July valence.

## The authorless reading is here too, and explicitly not a conspiracy

`FACEBOOK.md` amends the riff note's push on the strength of the July "Agent A /
Agent B" screenshot. May states it more cleanly, and in his own words rather than
a quoted source:

> **NOVED:** So this is like a conspiracy.
> **DEVON:** No, I think it's something between knee-jerk defense mechanism and
> improv. It's less about my narrative and more about preserving their own.

*Defense mechanism and improv.* That is §4b's authorless hybrid — coordinated
output with no coordinator, produced because each participant is protecting
something, not because anyone drafted a plan. And he reaches it by **explicitly
rejecting the authored alternative when it is offered to him.**

Two further formulations from the same document:

- On the smear's construction: *"I wouldn't say false, I would say it has the
  kernel of truth, but upon that is built a narrative that isn't accurate."*
- On its motive: *"preserving their own superiority, obfuscating perceptions of
  inferiority."*

## The scales are joined inside the transcript

The riff dates *"authoritarianism is interpersonal violence at scale"* to 2019 as
an insight arrived at by year's end. The transcript **performs** the move in May,
in a single exchange. NOVED raises Kremlin information warfare; DEVON supplies
Operation Trust; NOVED then pivots without a seam:

> **NOVED:** Speaking of disinformation campaigns, let's bring it back to you and
> this notion that there are actors out there spreading false information about
> you.

Elsewhere in the same piece he runs the argument the other way, from state to
person, via the crack-cocaine sentencing disparity — a false claim (*"crack is
100 times stronger than cocaine"*) that made law and outlived its own correction:

> Things don't have to be true to change other true things, and sometimes even
> true things can't do shit.

That is the thesis in operational form: a narrative with a kernel of truth,
built out, resistant to correction, producing consequences at whatever scale it
is running on. **The May transcript is where the interpersonal case and the
state case are first held in one frame.**

## Quantum tigers: fiction as a method for a mechanism you cannot aim at

**2019-05-30**, headed `CW: NPD traits`:

> I'm approaching this part like that book Mr. Tompkins by George Gamow. Where
> the title character attends boring lectures, falls asleep in them, and dreams
> worlds that illustrate the scientific concepts he's sleeping through. In this
> case, I'm trying to illustrate NPD folk by hunting them like quantum tigers.
>
> See, you try and shoot a quantum tiger with your rifle. Well, there's not one
> but several of them, flicking very quickly in each other's vicinity. And they
> are all the same tiger. But you don't shoot them straight on, instead, you have
> to shoot wildly in a random direction, and you just might hit them(?)

Two things at once. It is **the multiplicity model in his own coinage** — several
that are all the same one — and it is an **epistemic claim about method**: the
thing cannot be addressed head-on, so the approach has to be oblique.

And the frame is Gamow's: *use a fictional world to illustrate a mechanism the
audience cannot otherwise follow.* Seventeen months later, on 2020-10-02, he
encodes *"a perpetrated-perpetrator dichotomy, and as many tactics of deflection,
manipulation"* into an AI Dungeon **World Info** entry and runs scenarios against
it. Same method, new instrument. The World Info entry is not where the technique
starts; it is where the technique acquires a machine.

## The paranoia post: a disconfirming test, run in public, on himself

**2019-05-20**, 305 words. He takes a standard definition — *"Paranoia is
thinking and feeling as if you are under threat even though there is no (or very
little) evidence that you are"* — and builds a decision procedure from it, with
worked cases calibrated by social position: the plant worker (no), the man with a
side-job near the meth trade (*"depends on how good you are at your job"*), the
sculptor with a pending wrongful-death suit against the NYPD (*"maybe they want
to get information"*).

Then the last case turns the definition on itself:

> Is your husband a closeted sociopath? Well, then, your husband has already
> gotten you, turned your children against you, and all your friends think you
> are cuckoo […] But since there is probably very little evidence, people can call
> it paranoia anyway.

**The evidential test fails precisely where the mechanism has been most
successful**, because destroying the evidence is what the mechanism does. He
publishes this three days before the transcript in which he claims a perception-
management campaign is being run against him — that is, he states the strongest
argument against his own position first, in public, and then makes the claim
anyway with the qualification attached.

This is the disconfirming-test discipline `CLAUDE.md` asks of anyone working in
this repo, performed by the author on himself in 2019.

He applies the same scepticism to the literature that would flatter him:

> Most stuff on the internet is made by the victims or survivors. So there's a
> sinister quality to the accounts that may or may not have been there. […] But
> what I dislike even more, is the notion that there is no cure. Lots of articles
> end with that, and it's almost as if you can see the victim enacting revenge.
> […] I have to read all these accounts as both true and false.

## Triangulation given as an abstract mechanism, not an accusation

**2019-05-30**, 402 words. The tactic is set out with the parties reduced to
roles — *Person*, *Other*, *Third* — and analysed functionally: the Third is
recruited to work around boundaries the Other has established; the Other suppresses
their own anger so as not to burden the Third; the suppression eventually produces
*"the anxious and volatile behaviors that the Person had been telling the Third
about the whole time."*

The mechanism is **self-confirming**: it manufactures the evidence for its own
cover story. That is the same structure as the paranoia post's last case, and it
is why the July dump had to be documentary — the tactic's output is
indistinguishable from the accusation unless the record is produced.

## It independently corroborates the riff's chronology

The riff was dictated on 2026-08-13, seven years after the events. The May 2019
posts, written contemporaneously, give the same dates without being asked:

| riff, 2026 | May 2019 posts |
|---|---|
| *"2016 accountability procedures akin to metoo"* | *"February 2016 when the organization transition process began"* (05-31) |
| *"2017 to 2019 — crash course in coercive behavior"* | *"It certainly culminated in October 2017, when I became certain of the cognitive styles"* (05-31) |
| *"enough distance from the event"* | *"I extracted myself from those environments, and achieved a stability"* (05-28) |

The 05-28 post also dates the onset further back than the riff does — *"has since
2007, and maybe even stretching back to 1998, but not truly damaging until it
ramped up in 2012, and came to a head in 2017"* — and counts the same cohort the
transcript does: *"I count seven or eight folks in my life, maybe more."*

**The dictated riff is reliable where it can be checked.** That is worth recording
given how much of this repo now rests on reconstructions of phone dictation.

## What this changes

- **`READINGS.md` §IV needs a date.** The Counterfactual Interview is a 2019
  hand-built form with a stated lineage back to AOL auto-FAQs, not a technique
  discovered through language models. §IV's claim that it is "a technique for the
  powerless" is strengthened by this — it was built under duress, for use, before
  any tool made it easy.
- **§4b's authorless hybrid has a cleaner source than the July screenshot.** In
  May he rejects the conspiracy framing when it is handed to him and names the
  alternative himself: defense mechanism and improv.
- **The Oct 2020 World Info entry is the second instance of a method, not the
  first.** Gamow's dream-worlds come first.
- **The disconfirming standard is his before it is the repo's.**

## The scale thesis, written out on 2019-05-31

The riff dates *"authoritarianism is interpersonal violence at scale"* to 2019 as
something arrived at over the year. It is in the hypervigilance post, in those
words' substance, on **2019-05-31**:

> I stim because, as someone marked devalued, I have to not only do my dayjob,
> but I have to do the extra work of documenting potential problem behaviors […]
> **This is a tool of war, both on the international and interpersonal scales.**
> The very act of disagreement is considered the first volley of explosives, and
> the war, for them, does not end until you are discredited or destroyed.

The same post also contains **"Bad Faith", capitalised, as a term of art**:

> When my body senses that someone might be acting in Bad Faith, I just have to
> observe, listen, and take note.

`READINGS.md` §X is built on his enumeration of the bad-faith repertoire in the
2023-12 threads. The term is load-bearing in his own prose **four and a half
years earlier**, and already paired with the documentary response.

## The confiding trap — a real limit on this repo's own method

`analysis/MAY2019.md` above credits him with running disconfirming tests in
public. The hypervigilance post records what that discipline cost:

> When the body sounds the alarm, my head assesses the veracity of the signal.
> […] When it involves a loved one, I go to them and say, "Look, I think I might
> be having some distorted thoughts, so I wanna get it off my chest so that you
> can tell me how stupid I'm being, and that should bring relief." The first few
> times, I've managed to tell myself how stupid I'm being. **Which makes it all
> the worse when my initial alarm was actually pointing to danger, especially
> since the danger's origin was the very person I was confiding in.**

He built the correct procedure — check the perception against a trusted party —
and the procedure routed through the source of the harm, converting each check
into further evidence against himself.

This is worth carrying beyond the biography. `CLAUDE.md` makes the disconfirming
test load-bearing in this repo, and the standing instruction is to run one on
primed claims. **The May 2019 posts document the failure mode of that method: a
disconfirming test is only as good as the independence of what it tests
against.** He identified this in 2019 and the repo has never stated it.

## All abuse is physical — the argument that scales downward

The 1,059-word post of **2019-05-21** builds the same bridge from the other end,
by escalation. Verbal abuse is physical because *"the compressed air of sound
waves hit ear follicles […] and unleash fear chemicals"*; emotional abuse because
the conundrum *"imprisons the body"*. Then spatial:

> Let's think of this elderly woman, she's just been evicted! She lived there for
> 35 years. […] Her brain's place cells […] accumulated a very dense mental map in
> her home-space. When the city sent movers, and they hauled the boxes to the
> curb, the movers tore apart the mental map, which due to her dementia, was the
> only thing keeping her social faculties together. In a few days, she lost the
> ability to speak, and she died six months later.
>
> Abuse can take many forms, **even as an installation**. Even as a misremembered
> anecdote told the exact same wrong way. Even the chronology of party
> invitations.

*Even as an installation* is an arts administrator's word, and it is the whole
argument: **the movers intended nothing.** Harm is produced by an arrangement,
with no author at the point of delivery — the authorless mechanism reached from
the institutional side and landed in a single body. Where the transcript joins
the scales by analogy, this joins them by physics.

The post closes on the population case — *"this drug user in the presence of
communities of crocodile concern can kill you with population-wide nocebo. The
Drug War depends on this to survive"* — which is the nocebo article he had
posted on 05-13 saying *"I need to place this into the hive mind."* The May
series can be watched assembling its own sources.

## Multiplicity is claimed for the survivors, in his own words

`FACEBOOK.md` credits the survivor valence of multiplicity to a quoted source in
the July screenshots. It is his own, six weeks earlier, and tactical:

> We find ourselves on cam sites, where the 25 simultaneous feeds is too much for
> our abusers to process. The human thirst gazes at whom? At dozens. Our abusers
> know none of them, and told them no malignant stories.
>
> We can be ourselves. **For we are multiple, we might not be Legion, but we are
> a god-damned handful.**

Multiplicity as distributed attention, adopted because a single point of contact
is a single point of attack. The hypervigilance post repeats it — *"100 different
pairs of eyes, and all of them aren't on me. An ever-roving gaze"* — and the
2024-10-17 revision chain (*"Here have one of my selves […] Want to kill this
part of me? Fine, have it"*) is the same manoeuvre five years on.

## A rival explanation for §X's asymmetry

The same post states the small-set-of-shapes observation and then gives a cause
that §X does not have:

> once we mention the abuse, we can finish each other's sentences and identify
> the ones in the scene who exhibit the same traits. It. Is. Uncanny. […]
> **It's like all our abusers are the same abuser. That's because of THEIR
> wounds, inflicted before personhood began.**

§X argues bad faith has few shapes because it optimises under an extra constraint
— appear to cooperate while defecting. **This argues the shapes are few because
the formation is uniform: same injury, same age, same result.** Those are
different claims. The constraint account predicts convergence among unrelated
actors with no shared history; the etiological account predicts a shared history.
They are separable, and the corpus has evidence bearing on both. Neither
`READINGS.md` nor this file has tested either.

## The practice has an origin, and it is a prompt

The 603-word post of **2019-05-21** traces his writing to evangelical prayer —
*"Prayer wasn't about requests, it was a way to take my rage in my hand […]
Instead of clawing at my classmates because I couldn't touch them, I'd write
story fragments"* — and then to a CompuServe CB exchange at fifteen:

> He asked me to tell an erotic story. I slapped together details from Nifty
> erotic stories. Your general two people having sex in a basement, and someone
> in a vocation looking through a tiny crack in a door. A coach? **The person in
> the closet had an elaborate inner life.**
>
> "Are you sure you're 15?" But he logged off before I could respond.

Every structural element of the later corpus is present: **a remote interlocutor
issues a prompt; he generates erotic fiction by recombining an existing corpus of
text; the interlocutor evaluates and the exchange terminates.** That is the
turn-taking frame `FINDINGS.md` adopts as its unit of analysis, running around
1995 with a human on the other end and Nifty as the training set. The 2020-08-10
post supplies the machine half of the same lineage — *"Ever since tapping with
Eliza in the form of Freud on my Apple IIE"*.

It also seeds the corpus's recurring figure. His first erotic story has a hidden
observer with an interior life watching through a crack — the witness position
the Counterfactual Interview later formalises.

## The series stops mid-argument, and resumes as images

Two promises in the May series were never kept.

**`Transcript 1` has no `Transcript 2`**, noted already in `FACEBOOK.md`. And the
comic-relief post of 05-28 announces the next instalment in a bracketed aside:

> [I need to give some comic relief, and a sense that there's been growth from
> all this. **Next post is gonna be about dislike towards the construct of NPD
> Abuse as it has developed from 2014 to present**, cuz you know mental health
> language for the most part sucks.]

That post does not exist. The posts that follow are harm-reduction links, then
the quantum-tigers piece — which *illustrates* the traits rather than critiquing
the construct — and then the thread drops. **June 2019 collapses to 8 posts and
267 words**, most of them bare links. Six weeks later the July dump begins, and
it is almost entirely images.

So the shape of the summer is: an argument built in prose across three weeks,
announcing two continuations; both abandoned; near-silence for six weeks; then
the same case resumed as 157 screenshots with the diagnostic label struck out.
**The written half did not conclude — it stopped, and the documentary half is
what replaced it.** Whether that was exhaustion, a decision that prose was the
wrong instrument, or the events of late June that the screenshots are dated to,
the archive does not say.
