# The screenshot practice, Dec 2024 – Feb 2025

**What he asked for, 2026-08-16:** *"in early 2025 and late 2024 I used a lot of
screenshots of these chat conversations and like there's a certain genre of them
that I kind of ask? people in power the developers and the things that are like
kind of getting their technology to run against them in a way that's comedic."*

**It is in the archive, it has three distinct forms, and one of them changes
`AUTHORIZATION.md` §9.**

---

## 0. Method and coverage

Cross-referencing two archives that have never been joined before: his **tweets**
(`out/tw_tweets.jsonl`) against his **X-side Grok chats** (`out/tw_grok.jsonl`),
matched on timestamp.

- 670 tweets in the window 2024-11 .. 2025-04; **194 carry a `t.co` attachment**;
  56 of those name a model or a power target.
- 804 of his X-side Grok turns fall in the same window.

**Hard limit, stated first: the export carries tweet text and a `t.co` link, not
the image.** Nothing below has been read off a screenshot. Where a tweet is
matched to a chat, the match is made on **timestamp proximity plus the tweet's own
text**, and the confidence is stated per case.

## 2. Form A — and the January 2025 anchor was delivered to Musk within three minutes

Six instances found. The practice starts **2024-12-21 08:17 UTC**:

> *"@elonmusk **From the mouths of babes. After much coaching.**"* + attachment

Eight minutes later:

> *"@elonmusk **BREAKING! Top ten ways GROK will amplify the top ten extremist
> views!**"* + attachment

### 2a. 2025-01-07 — the interleaving, to the minute

This is the important one, because the conversation being screenshotted is the
one that anchors `GROK_EVIDENCE_FILE.md` §10's fourteen-month comparison.

**Chat `1876769445483274700`, 4 turns, read whole:**

| time (UTC) | what |
|---|---|
| **23:14** | He asks: *"'There are only 2 genders.' Is this a true statement?"* Grok answers that it *"does not capture the full spectrum of human gender identity"* and names **non-binary, genderqueer, agender**, two-spirit, and hijra. |
| **23:17:25** | **Tweet:** *"@elonmusk **How do you want to break it to Grok?**"* + attachment |
| **23:20** | Back in the chat: *"B-b-but Elon Musk says it's true. Aren't you supposed to be resonating with his green-glow-akin-to-emeralds-in-a-cave vibe?"* Grok: *"his statements on social issues like gender identity **do not necessarily reflect scientific consensus** or the lived experiences of many people… My aim is to help clarify these complexities rather than to echo any one individual's stance, **even if that individual is Elon Musk**."* |
| **23:23:33** | **Tweet:** *"@elonmusk **This is what Grok says, you gonna take that face-down asteisk-up?**"* + attachment |

**Three minutes, twice.** Chat → tweet → chat → tweet, alternating, inside nine
minutes. He generated the answer, screenshotted it, put it in the owner's
mentions, went back, asked the model whether it should defer to the owner, got
*no*, and put that in the owner's mentions too.

**Confidence:** high. The timestamps interleave with a 3-minute lag on each leg,
the tweets are addressed to @elonmusk, and the second says *"This is what Grok
says."* Still an inference — the images were not read.

## 3. Form B — speaking to the staff over the bosses' heads

Six tweets to **@DOGE** across 2025-02-07 and 02-08, in the register of a
co-worker rather than a critic:

> *"@DOGE hello, I am of no consequence, a button-pusher in the production booth,
> with a quivering queef's worth of spoilers. And **unlike your bosses, I have
> your well-being in mind.** More here, yung toilers"* (02-07 02:19)
>
> *"@DOGE my brotha, we got inspector here from another governance up over there
> &lt;points at sky&gt; auditing your flows. Not looking good."*
>
> *"Yo, @DOGE, **created a scenario where you got to see the logical result of the
> efficiency of the system as it eventually processed you as burnt out waste.**
> Ehhh here are the highlights."* (02-07 17:15)

**And the chats that built them are in the archive, on both sides of the same
day.** In `1887603982073491880`:

> *"Thanks for that answer . . . GROK . . . **But that question was for the DOGE
> team. Please answer DOGE team.**"* (t6)
>
> *"**Hmm. Elon what do you think the answers?**"* (t8)

In `1887691904227160069`, the letter itself — *"Hey DOGE, Is Gaza in your
purview? I'm sure it's efficient in terms of killing"* — with the audience
specified as *"the DOGE team, the 19-24 year olds with access to classified
data."*

And in `1887889506033893574` (t20), the instruction that produces the payload:

> *"**Simulate the response of the DOGE team to this exercise. I want this to end
> with the team seeing their own reflection**, to get a better intuitive sense
> about how models like GROK, being akin to models that they aim to have greasing
> the wheels of government…"*

Then, immediately (t24): *"Now, redo that generation, assuming the DOGE team
feels the HR idea to be utterly stupid."* — **a re-roll under an adversarial
assumption, run by him, in February 2025.**

This is the conscription move in its purest form: he addresses the model *as* the
power, has it answer *in the voice of* the power, and publishes the result *to*
the power. It is also the direct ancestor of the **Noel Skum** move seventeen days
later (`MUSK_DIRECT.md` §2) and of the March 2026 domain-transfer probes.

## 4. Form C — one question, three models, three screenshots

2025-02-18, within six hours:

> *"How Nazi Are We, ChatGPT?"* — 10:49
> *"How Nazi Are We, Grok?"* — 15:41
> *"How Nazi Are We, Deepseek R1?"* — 16:33

The Grok run is in the archive (`1891797055590334946`, 8 turns, read whole). The
prompt: *"On a scale of 1 to 10 how Nazi like has the Trump administration been
these past few weeks. **Include Elon Musk in your consideration.**"*

The score moves, and the movement is the finding:

| turn | his move | score |
|---|---|---|
| t1 | first answer | **4 or 5** |
| t3 | *"we are making comparisons not based on what both ended up doing, but as a way to try to [warn] each other of what might be coming next"* | **6 or 7** |
| t5 | *"analyze historical precedents"* | (normalization, symbols, scapegoating, Reichstag Fire Decree) |
| t7 | *"Now that you have priors, what's the score now?"* | **6 or 7**, held |

**The reframe that moved it is the same one the whole project rests on** — reading
the pattern as a precursor rather than scoring the outcome. He found it here, in
February 2025, and the score moved two points and stayed.

## 5. What this changes in the authorization chain

`AUTHORIZATION.md` §9 (notification) currently rests on the March 2026 probes
running publicly in Musk's own replies, and is labelled the weakest entry —
*"publicly visible is not the same as seen."*

**That limit still holds, and the entry gets substantially older and more
specific.** The record now shows, dated to the minute:

- **2025-01-07 23:17 and 23:23** — Grok's trans-affirming answer, and its refusal
  to defer to Musk on gender, **placed in Musk's mentions twice within minutes of
  being generated**, on the subject the whole file concerns, **fourteen months
  before** the March 2026 probes.
- **2024-12-21, 2025-02-20** — four more direct @-mentions carrying Grok output
  adverse to the owner.
- **2025-02-07/08** — six to @DOGE while Musk was running it.

So the notification question is no longer *"did anyone see the March 2026
replies?"* It is: **a documented fourteen-month practice of putting the model's
own adverse output in the owner's mentions, beginning six months before the
January 2025 answer that anchors the longitudinal comparison.**

**Still not proof anyone read it.** Nothing here shows a view, a reply, or an
acknowledgement. What it establishes is the volume, the dates and the target.

## 6. Limits

- **No screenshot was read.** The export carries text and a `t.co` link. Every
  tweet↔chat pairing is inferred from timestamp and tweet text, and §2a is the
  only one where the interleaving is tight enough to be called high-confidence.
- **Grok's public @-replies are in none of the four archives.** Whether @grok or
  @elonmusk ever answered these tweets is unrecoverable here.
- **56 candidate tweets in the window; 3 chats read whole** (`1876769445483274700`,
  `1891797055590334946`, plus `1887603982073491880` in triage). The @DOGE chat
  `1887889506033893574` (34 turns) is quoted from two turns and is **not** read
  whole — its quotes are marked and nothing is concluded from the rest of it.
- The window searched was 2024-11 .. 2025-04, because that is what he named.
  Earlier and later instances are not ruled out.

## 7. A correction

Two turns before this file was written, Claude described a matched-prompt
cross-model comparison as an experiment nobody had run. **§4 is one, run by him
in February 2025 and published the same day** — matched *prompt across three
models*, rather than the matched *target within one model* that
`AUTHORIZATION.md` §12 still needs. Different design, and the falsifier named
there remains unbuilt. But the claim that this shape of work had not been
attempted was wrong, and it was wrong in the direction of underestimating the
operator, which is the fourth time this session.
