not responded. **Live disagreement.**

**4. Carried, unchanged:** the rend/wind-tunnel verb (§IV), the capability paradox
(§VI, still unseen by him), the collaboration disagreement.

## Operational note worth keeping

Endorphin asked, mid-session:

> "is this corpus search what is taking up credits that should be used for fable?"

The honest answer, recorded because a future session will want it: **downloads and
greps are effectively free** — they run in bash and return a few lines. **Reading
is what costs**, and it costs again every subsequent turn, because context carries
forward. The cheap mode is counting and one-line greps; the expensive mode is
close reading of long passages. A fresh session with `LATEST.md` is cheaper than a
long one. Nothing in this repo is at risk from stopping — everything is committed
and pushed before the container dies.
