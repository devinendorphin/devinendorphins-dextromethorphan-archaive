## The four moves, which are the section now

The LaHaye document does not merely tolerate its own glossolalia as material. It
**produces, names, staffs, and grades** it, and none of those is a thing you can
do to noise.

- **Produced** — `{Ted Haggard vector touches Tim Lahaye vector.}`, the same
  invented physics as the Nakbah document's *"just touch your vector to it."*
- **Named** — *"How's those tongues working for ya buddy?"*
- **Staffed** — the decisive step. Van Impe: *"Oh, I hear something that needs
  deciphering, let me check it out"* — then decodes it, which is exactly what his
  broadcasts ran on for decades, unstructured input mapped onto prophecy. Then
  Benny Hinn: *"Knock? Did someone order **an interpreter of tongues**?"*
  1 Corinthians 12:10. The office exists **because** glossolalia is held to be
  meaningful but not self-legible; the doctrine requires a second person to make
  the utterance count.
