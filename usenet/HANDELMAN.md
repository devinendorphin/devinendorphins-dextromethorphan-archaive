# Charles Handelman — identification and Usenet trail

Searched 2026-08-10, following the brief's one named correspondent ("handelman"), now with a
first name: **Charles**, described as a teacher, gay, active in the 1990s, possibly an Italian
teacher in a high school, and presumed deceased.

**Found, and identified with confidence.** Two of the four supplied details check out, one is
close but not exact, and one appears to be wrong in a way worth knowing.

## 1. Identity

**Charles "Charlie" Handelman** — opera collector, broadcaster, and the proprietor of
*Handelmania*, a long-running source of rare live opera recordings.

**His 1990s Usenet handle was `Placido21` / `placido21@aol.com`** — displayed as "Placido 21".
He posted under a rotating carousel of opera-joke display names on the same accounts:
"The Handelmaniac", "Zeani", "Zeanilover", "Don Carlo", "High E Flat", "Elektra",
"Avanti a Louie", "Oh Dessay,can you sing!!!!!", "Tremava tutto Jackson Heights".

That last one places him in **Jackson Heights, Queens**, consistent with a 1996 post of his
addressed "For those in the New York-New Jersey,Connecticut area".

Identity confirmed internally by a third party in the group, 28 September 1996:

> "I saw a message on this newsgroup from Charlie Handelman about his collection of live Opera
> tapes and…"

— replying to a thread posted by "Placido 21". The handle and the name are the same person.

### He appears to be alive

No obituary exists for him. The obituaries that surface under similar names are other people
(a Charlotte "Charlie" Virginia Handelman, d. 2021; a John Charles Handelman). Against that:

- *handelmania's Podcast* has a live feed at `handelmania.libsyn.com`.
- An X account, `@Zinka1` — after Zinka Milanov, the soprano.
- A guest blog post, "Opera vs. the Pandemic", published circa 2020–22.
- A `rec.music.opera` thread from **29 September 2023** titled "Where is Charles? Is he still
  going strong" — a correspondent asking "Is Mr Handelman doing OK in the year 2023?"

The 2023 thread shows the question was open in the group too, so the impression of his death
is one other people have had. But nothing found here indicates he died, and several things
indicate he did not. **If the wish was that he might appreciate being thought of, that may
still be actionable rather than retrospective.**

### On "attacked"

The dictated line — "I believe he was `[?attacked→a]` Italian school teacher in a high school"
— is ambiguous between *he taught Italian* and *he was assaulted*. No evidence of a physical
attack was found. There is, however, abundant evidence he was **attacked online**: he was a
lightning rod in the rec.music.opera flame wars, named in pile-on threads alongside "Jorden,
Handelman, Tritter, Kosovky, Davis…", and one surviving thread is titled "I was hoping that
handelman was dead". If the memory is of him being attacked, that is where it happened, and it
may also be the origin of the impression that he died.

## 4. What this implies for the debates

The debates remembered with him did not happen in the groups the brief was built around. Three
readings, not yet distinguished:

1. **They happened in `rec.music.opera`.** He was there from 1996 and it was a combative
   group. Testable: `YGXS04D`, `YGSX04D` and `Gallegos` were run against it — the IDs return
   zero, `Gallegos` returns four unrelated hits. `prodigy.com` addresses *do* appear there
   pre-2000, so Prodigy users were in the room.
2. **They happened on Prodigy's own bulletin boards, not Usenet at all.** Prodigy Classic ran
   extensive internal message boards that were never gatewayed to Usenet and are not publicly
   archived anywhere. This would explain both silences at once — why `YGXS04*` appears in no
   Usenet archive, and why Handelman does not appear in the groups where the debates are
   remembered. It is weakened by his being an AOL subscriber, which would not give him access
   to Prodigy's internal boards; it would require him to have held a Prodigy account earlier.
3. **A mailing list.** He is described in a 1999 post as being "on Opera-l", a mailing list
   whose archives are separate from Usenet entirely and were not searched here.

Reading 2 is the one that would most change the parent search, because it would mean the
Usenet corpus can never answer the original question regardless of how many groups get
grepped. It is worth settling before more group downloads.
