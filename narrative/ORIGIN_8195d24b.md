These are in the record, and a viewer who reads the share link will find them.

1. **The headline numbers are Claude's estimates, not sourced counts.** The 200M–1B subgraph is
   built from assumptions such as *"Conservatively, each affects a local population of
   5,000–50,000."* The ~70,000 deciders is a sum of round guesses (*"estimated ~50,000 people…
   ~10,000… ~5,000… ~few thousand"*). By msg 19 both appear as plain fact: *"The decision to
   find these costs acceptable is being made by roughly 70,000 people."* That is the same

   boundary card.

   Self-Witness opens on the second overstatement: *"A record of our conversations persists and
